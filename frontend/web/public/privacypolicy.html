Repwise Privacy Policy

Repwise helps you track workouts, nutrition, and daily steps. This notice explains how health-related data is used on your device.

Health data

When you enable phone or wearable step sync, Repwise reads step counts from Android Health Connect (and HealthKit on iOS). Steps may include data from your phone sensors and paired wearables that sync to Health Connect.

Step totals are uploaded to your Repwise account so they appear in Home and Progress. We do not sell health data. You can revoke Health Connect access at any time in system settings, and you can turn off sync in Profile.

Notifications

With your permission, Repwise can show an ongoing notification while a workout session is active so you can see the timer from the notification shade.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through your Repwise support channel.
